How It Works

From sign-up to
first booking.

Every owner follows the same verified onboarding. Once approved, your listings reach thousands of active businesses instantly.

01
Register and Verify
Fill your business profile. Submit your GST number and company registration. Admin verifies within 2–3 business days — no paperwork, fully digital.
02
List Your Billboards
List each billboard separately. Upload day and night photos, enter dimensions, pincode-based auto city fill, traffic footfall data, and set your monthly pricing.
03
Browse and Bid on Tenders
Businesses post anonymous campaign budgets daily. Browse tenders that match your locations and formats. Submit tailored proposals — no cold calling, ever.
04
Respond to Quote Requests
Businesses directly request quotes on your listings. Respond with your best pricing and availability — the client sees only your proposal, not your identity.
05
Close the Deal and Get Paid
When a business selects your bid, their contact is revealed. Finalise pricing, sign your agreement, and mark it live on your dashboard. Revenue tracked automatically.

FAQ

Real questions.
Straight answers.

What if I don't get any bids or quote requests?
The most common reason for low leads is incomplete or low-quality listings — missing photos, no traffic data, or vague location. Our team reviews every new listing and sends you specific suggestions to improve visibility. Over 90% of owners who complete their listing fully receive a quote request within their first week.
What documents do I need to register?
You need: GST registration certificate (mandatory), company registration or sole proprietorship certificate, day and night photographs of each billboard, and your bank account details for future payouts. Everything is uploaded digitally — no physical documents required.
How does the tender anonymity work — both ways?
When a business posts a tender, their company name is hidden from you — you only see the industry, budget, location, and format. When you submit a bid, the business sees your portfolio and pricing but not your name. Contact details are exchanged only after a deal is confirmed by both sides.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your dashboard settings. Your listings remain live until the end of your current billing cycle — we don't remove them immediately. If you cancel, you lose access to new tender bids, but existing client relationships continue unaffected.
Does AdBasket take a cut when I close a deal?
No commission whatsoever. Our entire business model is subscription-based. When you close a deal — whether it's ₹50,000 or ₹50 Lakhs — 100% of that money goes to you. We make money from monthly subscriptions, not from your success.
I'm in a Tier 2 city — are there tenders for me?
Absolutely. Our fastest-growing cities in 2026 are Jaipur, Nagpur, Coimbatore, Kochi, and Indore. Many pan-India brands — FMCG, EdTech, Banking — specifically look for Tier 2 inventory because competition is lower and recall is higher. Tier 2 owners often win faster than metro owners.
What happens if a business doesn't pay after the deal is closed?
AdBasket facilitates the introduction — the payment agreement is between you and the business directly. We strongly recommend using a standard OOH booking agreement (we provide a template) and collecting an advance before campaign launch. Every business on the platform has a verified company identity.
How long does verification actually take?
We promise 2–3 business days, and in practice it's usually done within 24 hours on weekdays. You'll get an email the moment your account is approved. If there's an issue with any document, our team contacts you directly with specific instructions — no vague rejection notices.
